# 栗子森林 Chestnut Mora Brand Guide

## Brand identity

| Field | Approved direction |
| --- | --- |
| Brand name | 栗子森林 |
| English name | Chestnut Mora |
| Instagram | `@chestnut_mora` |
| Main product language | 萌栗 |
| Product categories | 手機鍊、手機吊飾、包包吊飾、鑰匙圈、延伸式角色小配件 |
| Brand promise | 把喜歡的角色帶進每天的生活。 |
| Primary CTA | 逛逛最新萌栗 |
| Secondary CTA | 追蹤 @chestnut_mora |

## Positioning

栗子森林是一個以「正版角色 × 手作串珠 × 角色收藏 × 日常生活」為核心的手作
品牌。品牌不是單純販售手機鍊，而是把喜歡的角色、珠珠與配件搭配成可以每天
帶出門的小收藏。

網站的第一印象應該是「一座收藏喜歡的小小森林」，而不是一般電商商品頁。
商品照片與生活情境是主角，介面只負責讓人理解、喜歡並前往 Instagram。

## Core message system

### Primary

> 把喜歡的小角色，掛進每天的日常。

### Secondary

> 正版角色 × 手作搭配

### Supporting copy

- 每一條萌栗，都有自己的小小世界。
- 小小的萌栗，裝著大大的喜歡。
- 萌栗，陪你去很多地方。
- 每一個平凡的日子，都有萌栗陪著你。

Do not repeat the same sentence in every section. Use the primary message in the
Hero, then pace supporting lines across About, Lifestyle, Story, and Instagram CTA.

## Personality

The site should feel:

- 溫柔、可愛、具有收藏感
- 手作、日系生活、小森林、咖啡牛奶
- cozy, handcrafted, collectible, warm, premium, emotional, soft editorial
- Cute but not childish

The tone is calm and inviting. Write in Traditional Chinese first. Keep copy specific,
short, and easy to scan on a phone. Use small English editorial phrases only as accents,
never as a substitute for important information.

## Visual thesis

**Warm Editorial Boutique × Japanese Lifestyle × Handmade Collectible Studio × Soft
Forest Story**

The page silhouette is built from generous whitespace, large lifestyle photography,
small detail photography, restrained handwritten accents, and sparse forest/chestnut
decorations. The decoration should create atmosphere; it must never compete with the
product or become an illustration system repeated in every section.

Use 80% cream and brown as the visual base. Pink, sage, and olive are accents only.
Use supplied photography in a way that keeps the object physically believable: the
萌栗 should be connected to a phone, bag, tray, or other real object and should not look
like it is floating.

Do not use pure black as primary text. Do not use all accent colors heavily in the same
view. Avoid rainbow gradients, neon, high-saturation backgrounds, giant gradient blobs,
or generic blue-purple startup gradients.

## Type direction

- Traditional Chinese is the primary language.
- Primary typeface: [Huninn](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Huninn) for Traditional
  Chinese headings, body copy, navigation, and CTA labels.
- Fallbacks: Noto Sans TC, PingFang TC, Microsoft JhengHei, and the system sans-serif
  stack so the layout remains readable if the web font is unavailable.
- Handwritten type: only for short English phrases such as `made with ♡ in Chestnut
  Forest`.
- Mobile Hero H1: `clamp(36px, 10vw, 46px)`.
- Section H2: 28–32px on mobile.
- Body: 15–17px; small body 14px; captions 12–13px.
- Heading line-height: 1.25–1.4; body line-height: 1.65–1.85.
- Avoid excessive letter spacing in Chinese. Use manual semantic line breaks when a
  heading needs editorial pacing.

## What to avoid

Do not make the brand look like Shopee, Taobao, Pinduoduo, a dense ecommerce grid,
a sale banner, a children's cartoon site, a doll marketplace, an AI SaaS landing page,
or a generic template. Avoid large sale badges, red discount text, plastic 3D icons,
heavy shadows, glassmorphism, excessive rounded cards, random stock imagery, excessive
emoji, aggressive motion, and UI that adds complexity without helping the brand story.

## Claims and trust

Safe claims currently supported by the brief are: 正版角色、手工搭配／製作、小量
製作、用心包裝、售後服務. Do not upgrade these into unverified claims such as
official distributor, exclusive design, globally unique, permanent durability,
waterproof, lifetime warranty, or guaranteed satisfaction.

The FAQ and service details in `CONTENT.md` are user-approved brand copy. Keep their
warm, conversational tone and do not replace them with a repeated generic disclaimer.
